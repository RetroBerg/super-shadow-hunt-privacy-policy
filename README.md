# Privacy Policy — Super Shadow Hunt

**Developer:** Svartberg Games (solo developer)
**Effective date:** August 19, 2026
**Contact:** svartberggames@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information Super Shadow Hunt ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. It applies to the App as distributed on Google Play and any associated backend services.

We are a solo-developer studio, not a registered company. "Svartberg Games" is a trading name used for this project.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

We collect the minimum data needed to run the game. There are two tiers, depending on how you choose to play:

### 1.1 Default / Guest Play (always collected)
| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Username** | Displayed to other players during matches (Hunter/Hider sessions); required for basic gameplay identification. |
| **Device ID** | Used to create and recognize your guest account so your progress, inventory, and Credits persist on the same device between sessions. |

This data is collected automatically when you first launch the App — no signup form is required to play.

### 1.2 Optional Account Protection
If you choose to link an account (so your progress carries over to a new device), we additionally collect:

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Email address** | Used to identify and recover your linked account. |
| **Password** | Stored in hashed/salted form (never stored or transmitted in plain text) to authenticate you. |

This step is entirely optional and is only triggered by your explicit choice in the App's account settings.

### 1.3 Advertising Data (AdMob)
Super Shadow Hunt uses **Google AdMob** to serve two types of ads:

| Ad type | When it appears | User choice |
|---|---|---|
| **Interstitial ads** | Automatically, every 3 matches | Mandatory — not user-triggered, cannot be individually declined (aside from any future ad-removal option) |
| **Rewarded video ads** | Offered at the end of a match, in exchange for bonus Credits | Optional — only shown if you choose to watch one |

For both ad types, Google and its advertising partners may collect:

- Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier)
- General device and app-interaction data used for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention

We do not control what Google/AdMob collects on our behalf — this is governed by [Google's own Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [AdMob's data disclosure](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543). Because interstitial ads are shown automatically as part of normal play, some advertising data is collected even if you never interact with a rewarded ad. You can reset or limit your device's Advertising ID in your device's system settings.

### 1.4 Analytics & Crash Data (Firebase)
Super Shadow Hunt uses **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (integrated via the Godot × Firebase plugin) to understand app usage and diagnose crashes. This may include:

- App usage events (e.g., session length, screens/menus visited, feature usage such as match completions)
- Device/OS information (model, OS version, app version, language, general region)
- Crash logs and diagnostic stack traces if the app crashes or encounters an error

This data is processed by Google (Firebase) on our behalf and is used solely to improve app stability and understand how the game is played — not to identify you personally. See [Google's Firebase privacy documentation](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) for how Firebase handles this data.

### 1.5 What We Do NOT Collect
We do not collect: real names, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, or photos. We do not collect or store payment card details directly — any future purchases would be handled entirely by Google Play Billing.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- To operate core gameplay (matchmaking, sessions, progress/inventory persistence)
- To let you recover your account across devices, if you opted into account protection
- To serve ads (via AdMob) — both the automatic interstitial ads shown every 3 matches and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch
- To measure app usage and diagnose crashes (via Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics), so we can fix bugs and improve the game
- To respond to support or data-deletion requests

We do **not** sell your data, and we do not use it for purposes beyond those listed above.

---

## 3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Gameplay data (username, device ID, email, hashed password, inventory/progress) is stored on servers we operate ourselves (self-hosted Nakama backend on Hetzner Cloud infrastructure, located in the EU). Hetzner acts as our infrastructure provider (data processor) and does not have independent access to use your data for its own purposes.

Advertising, analytics, and crash data (Sections 1.3 and 1.4) is processed by **Google** (AdMob and Firebase), acting as a data processor under its own security and privacy safeguards.

---

## 4. Data Retention

- **Guest accounts:** retained as long as the App remains installed and the device ID is active; may be purged after prolonged inactivity.
- **Linked accounts:** retained until you request deletion or delete your account via the App.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time, in either of two ways:

1. **In-app:** Settings → Account → Delete My Data
2. **By email:** send a deletion request to **svartberggames@gmail.com**. We will process verified requests within 30 days.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you additionally have the right, under GDPR, to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Request a portable copy of your data
- Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact **svartberggames@gmail.com**.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Super Shadow Hunt is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at **svartberggames@gmail.com** so we can delete it.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data:

- All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit via **TLS/SSL**.
- Passwords are stored in hashed/salted form and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
- Access to production servers is restricted to the developer.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
